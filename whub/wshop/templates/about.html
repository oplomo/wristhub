Our Story

From a simple idea to a trusted watch destination

Wrist Hub started with a single belief: buying a watch online should feel as confident as walking into a premium boutique. Founded in Nairobi, we set out to create a space where craftsmanship meets transparency, and where every customer — no matter their budget or experience — can find a timepiece that feels made for them.

What began as a small curated collection has grown into a go-to destination for men’s, women’s, luxury, sport, and classic watches. Along the way, we have stayed true to the same promise: present every product honestly, package every order with care, and treat every customer like a returning friend.

Mission & Vision

Why we do what we do

Our mission is to make premium timepieces accessible, trustworthy, and enjoyable to buy — no guesswork, no hidden markups, no surprises.

We envision a Kenya where anyone can discover a watch that matches their personality, from the boardroom executive to the weekend adventurer, without compromising on authenticity or service. Every watch we list is selected for quality, durability, and honest value.

Our Journey

Milestones that shaped us

1

The Beginning

Wrist Hub was launched with a handpicked selection of watches and a simple website, driven by the desire to offer Kenyan buyers a safer, smarter way to shop for timepieces online.

2

Growing the Collection

We expanded into luxury, sport, dress, diver, and chronograph categories, partnering with more trusted brands and building a catalogue that fits every personality and occasion.

3

Putting Customers First

We introduced cash on delivery, free strap sizing, a 7-day exchange policy, and a minimum 3-month warranty — because great service is just as important as a great watch.

4

Building Community

Through our journal, gallery, and social channels, we now share styling guides, care tips, and behind-the-scenes moments that turn customers into a community of watch lovers.
